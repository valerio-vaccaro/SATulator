Bitcoin / fiat converter for ESP32

SATulator

A compact touchscreen converter for bitcoin, satoshis, and supported fiat currencies.

Build your device

What it does

SATulator runs on the ESP32-2432S028 (2.8-inch CYD). It downloads public exchange prices over Wi-Fi, calculates average and median rates, and stores prices and preferences in flash for later use.

Conversion screen

Built for a 240 × 320 touchscreen

Select a currency, direction, and unit; enter an amount; then choose average, median, or a custom BTC rate. Saved rates and settings keep the display useful offline.
